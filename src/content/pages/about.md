Welcome to the Tocqueville Liberty Institute, where our mission is to revitalize America’s commitment to the principles of liberal democracy. We believe that a well-informed and engaged citizenry is the cornerstone of a thriving republic. Our goal is to educate and inspire Americans about our nation’s economic journey, civic responsibilities, and the foundational principles that underpin our democratic institutions.

## Mission

The Tocqueville Liberty Institute is a 501(c)(3) whose mission is to revitalize
America's commitment to the principles of liberal democracy by educating and
inspiring Americans about our nation's economic journey, civic responsibilities,
and the foundations of our republic. Through workshops, talks, community
engagement, and multimedia outreach, we aim to empower Americans with the
knowledge and tools necessary to actively participate in and preserve our
democratic institutions.

## Vision

We envision an America where every citizen is an informed and engaged steward of
our democratic principles, actively contributing to the strength and resilience
of our republic.

## Core values

**Civic Education.** Promoting a deep understanding of American history,
government, and economics.

**Active Citizenship.** Encouraging participation in democratic processes at all
levels.

**Critical Thinking.** Fostering the ability to analyze information and make
informed decisions.
